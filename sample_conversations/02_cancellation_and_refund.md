# Cancellation Eligibility and Refund Follow-up

## What this illustrates

- Distinguishing “Can I cancel?” from an actual cancellation request
- Explaining partial cancellation eligibility
- Requiring confirmation before a consequential action
- Tracking cancellation and refund as separate processes

## Conversation

**User:** Can I cancel my latest order?

**Assistant:** Your latest order is **ORD-1087**. It contains:

1. UrbanTrail backpack — not yet shipped
2. SteelSip bottle — already shipped

The backpack can still be cancelled, but the bottle cannot. Would you like to cancel the backpack?

**User:** How much would I get back?

**Assistant:** The refund would be **₹2,499**, including the backpack’s allocated delivery charge. It would be returned to your UPI account within **3–5 business days** after cancellation is approved.

**User:** Okay, cancel it.

**Assistant:** Please confirm: cancel **1 UrbanTrail backpack** from **ORD-1087** for an expected refund of **₹2,499**?

**User:** Confirm.

**Assistant:** Your cancellation request has been submitted. The request number is **CAN-5521**. The SteelSip bottle will still be delivered tomorrow.

**User:** Has the refund happened already?

**Assistant:** Not yet. The cancellation is currently **awaiting seller approval**, so the refund has not been initiated. I’ll show it as “refund initiated” only after the cancellation is approved.
