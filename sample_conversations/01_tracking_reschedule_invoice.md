# Tracking, Rescheduling, and Invoice

## What this illustrates

- Identifying an order from a product reference
- Remembering the active order across multiple requests
- Confirming a delivery change before applying it
- Switching from delivery management to invoice generation
- Resolving a reference to a different order

## Conversation

**User:** Where is my headset?

**Assistant:** Your Nova wireless headset from order **ORD-1042** has shipped. It is expected to arrive on **Friday, August 21**, between **10 AM and 2 PM**.

**User:** Can I move that to Friday?

**Assistant:** Yes. The available Monday slot is **August 24, 2–6 PM**. Would you like me to reschedule the delivery?

**User:** Yes. Also, can I get the invoice?

**Assistant:** Done—delivery for **ORD-1042** is now scheduled for **Monday, August 24, 2–6 PM**.
